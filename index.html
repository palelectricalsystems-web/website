About the company

One partner, complete infrastructure — since 2020.

Pal Electrical Systems is a Jaipur-based integrated engineering company delivering intelligent, safe and future-ready infrastructure for hospitality, commercial, residential, industrial and institutional projects.

From concept to commissioning and long-term maintenance, we carry single-point accountability across Electrical, ELV, IT & Networking, Fire & Life Safety, Security and Solar — so clients coordinate with one partner instead of six vendors.

The practice is led by Satish Pal, who brings 6+ years of hands-on experience across electrical, ELV, IT and networking systems for hospitality, commercial and residential sectors.
